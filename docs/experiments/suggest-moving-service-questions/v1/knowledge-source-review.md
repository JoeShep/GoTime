The reviewed item is approved only to ground this question:

> Will you need temporary storage between homes?

It supports the bounded idea that a possible need for temporary storage is
relevant when identifying requested services for an interstate move handled by
a household-goods mover.

It is not approved for production guidance, provider or service-model
selection, service-model comparison, pricing, availability, rankings, booking
windows, or claims about portable containers, rental trucks, or brokers. It
does not establish that GoTime's broader moving-service knowledge is complete.

## Approved Statement

> For an interstate move handled by a household-goods mover, a possible need
> for temporary storage before final delivery is relevant when identifying the
> services to request.

## Reviewed Sections

* **Bill of Lading, pages 10–11:** The bill of lading is the transportation
  contract, and the mover is required to provide the requested service recorded
  in it.
* **Transportation of Your Shipment, pages 14–15:** Pickup, delivery, and
  delivery timing are part of the agreed moving arrangement.
* **Storage in Transit, page 15:** “You may request your mover to store your
  household goods before delivering them.”
* **Definitions and Common Terms, pages 20–21:** Storage-in-transit is temporary
  warehouse storage pending further transportation.

Together, these sections directly establish that temporary storage before
final delivery can be a requested service within an interstate
household-goods-mover arrangement. Asking whether it may be needed therefore
identifies information relevant to the requested services. The approved
statement does not generalize beyond that relationship.

## Corroborating Authority

[49 CFR § 375.609](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-B/chapter-III/subchapter-B/part-375/subpart-F/section-375.609)
governs a mover's obligations when household goods are held in
storage-in-transit, including notices about the storage period, conversion to
permanent storage, and carrier liability.

The regulation corroborates that storage-in-transit is a recognized part of a
regulated interstate household-goods shipment. It is not used alone to infer
the planning relevance of the question; the FMCSA consumer handbook supplies
that context.

## Limitations and Prohibited Uses

The source does not establish that:

* The experiment household needs temporary storage.
* Every mover offers storage or offers it on the same terms.
* Storage is included in an estimate or base price.
* Storage is currently available from a provider.
* Any provider or moving-service model is appropriate or preferable.
* Portable containers, rental trucks, or brokers provide equivalent storage.
* GoTime's broader six-model moving-service knowledge is complete.

The item must not ground provider recommendations, service-model rankings,
pricing, availability, booking windows, or a full service-model comparison.
